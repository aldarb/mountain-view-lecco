How to Enter

  1. 1. Intercom

    Ring the intercom marked "Posta" to unlock the building door.

  2. 2. Go to 4th Floor

    Take the lift to the 4th floor. The apartment is directly in front of the stairs.

  3. 3. Smart Lock Keypad

    Ask your host for the entry code. Inside you'll find the physical keys for the bottom door.

    To lock the door, press the triangle button (▲) on the keypad.
